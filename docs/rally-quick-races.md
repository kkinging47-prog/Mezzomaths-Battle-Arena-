# Short Rally races

The personal Rally setup now offers Race length:

| Mode | Questions | Distance | Course access | Cup rewards |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| Quick race | 5 | 600 m | Any course | None |
| Quick race | 10 | 900 m | Any course | None |
| Circuit Cup | 5–55 by stage | 1,200 m | Existing stage progression | Existing stars/unlocks |

Five-question quick practice is the default for a fresh setup. The selected mode is retained when changing race details, retrying or going to another course. Switching from a later quick course to a locked Cup course returns the selection to Stage 1. Question availability is checked against the chosen length. Both quick modes use the selected class/curriculum/topic and the existing bank validation.

All questions must be answered before reaching the finish line. The shorter distance applies to the player, computer drivers, 2D track and both 3D renderers. Existing Cup and teacher races default to 1,200 metres. Quick results save to account history with their mode label; they do not add Cup stars, unlock stages or unlock garage items. Existing account history without a mode is displayed as Circuit Cup.

Teacher assignments retain their chosen question counts, courses and timers. Personal quick-race controls do not change class assignments.

Validation covers five/ten-question selection, short finish lines, the required-answer gate, opponent distance limits, Cup defaults, saved history labels and real controller checks that quick races on later courses produce only a history patch. Existing teacher-race, account-progress and race-engine regression tests pass, along with the production build.
