# River Rally

Open from Games & live battles on the homepage, or `/#river-rally`.

Original illustrated river rowing game inspired by the supplied river-distance activity. The first course uses checkpoints at 495, 950, 1600, 1950, 2495 and 3000 metres. Five courses contain 6/8/10/12/14 generated questions, with three number-difficulty settings. Questions cover addition, subtraction, remaining distances, differences, rower masses, estimation and two-step reasoning.

All boats stop during questions and explanations. Correct answers award power and a timed speed boost. During rowing, hold Row/Space, steer with Left/Right or A/D, collect gold energy buoys and avoid logs. Escape, window blur and tab hiding pause the game. Complete every question and reach the finish with at least 70% correct to unlock the next course; race position is reported separately. Next course preserves difficulty. Results include every question's answer and method.

Unlocks and best accuracy are stored only on the current device, separately by difficulty; no database or account permissions change. No sign-in or question-bank connection is required. The content is procedurally generated arithmetic, not a curriculum bank assignment. The reference photograph is not published.

Checks: `node tests/river-rally-engine.test.mjs`, `node tests/river-rally-renderer.test.mjs`, `npm run build`.
